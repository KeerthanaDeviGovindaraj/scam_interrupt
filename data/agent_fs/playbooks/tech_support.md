# Tech support scam

A pop-up or caller claims the computer is infected and charges for fake repairs or 'refunds' an overpayment.

## Warning words
computer, virus, microsoft, apple support, refund, pop-up, popup, remote, technician, hacked computer

## What to ask the family
Ask {first}: “Is someone helping you with your computer or a refund right now?”

## What to teach the customer
Real tech companies don't call you about viruses or refunds. Never let a stranger connect to your computer or send money to fix it.
