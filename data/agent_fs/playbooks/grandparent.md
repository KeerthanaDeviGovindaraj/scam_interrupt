# Grandparent emergency scam

Someone pretends to be a grandchild or relative in trouble (jail, accident, hospital) and asks for money fast and in secret.

## Warning words
grandson, granddaughter, grandchild, nephew, niece, bail, jail, arrested, accident, hospital, lawyer, attorney, don't tell, not to tell, new number

## What to ask the family
Ask {first}: did she call her grandchild back on their usual number?

## What to teach the customer
Scammers often pretend to be a grandchild in trouble and ask you to keep it secret. Before sending money, hang up and call them back on the number you already have.
