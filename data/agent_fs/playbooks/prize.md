# Prize or lottery scam

The customer is told they won something but must pay fees or taxes first.

## Warning words
won, winner, prize, lottery, sweepstakes, jackpot, claim fee, processing fee

## What to ask the family
Ask {first}: “Were you told you won something?”

## What to teach the customer
If you have to pay to receive a prize, it isn't a prize. Real winnings never cost you money.
