# Romance scam

Someone met online builds a relationship, then needs money for travel, medical bills or an emergency, but never meets in person.

## Warning words
boyfriend, girlfriend, met online, dating, fiance, fiancé, overseas, soldier, plane ticket, customs, love

## What to ask the family
Ask {first}: has she met this person in real life?

## What to teach the customer
Someone you've only met online who asks for money is very often a scammer, even after months of talking.
