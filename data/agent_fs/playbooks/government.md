# Government impersonation scam

Someone claims to be from the IRS, Social Security, Medicare or the police and demands payment.

## Warning words
irs, tax, taxes, social security, medicare, police, warrant, fine, government, court, deported

## What to ask the family
Ask {first}: which agency called, and did they demand payment by transfer or gift card?

## What to teach the customer
Government agencies never demand immediate payment by transfer, gift card or crypto. Hang up and call the agency on its official number.
