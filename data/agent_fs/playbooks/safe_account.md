# “safe account” bank impersonation scam

A caller pretends to be the bank's fraud team and tells the customer to move money to a 'safe' account.

## Warning words
safe account, fraud department, fraud team, bank told me, protect my money, account is hacked, account was hacked, account hacked, move my money, secure account, keep it safe

## What to ask the family
Ask {first}: “Did someone saying they’re from the bank tell you to move money?”

## What to teach the customer
Your bank will never ask you to move money to a “safe account.” If someone does, hang up and call the number on the back of your card.
